You are playing a puzzle game where you can place portals to teleport between locations.

Portal Rules:
- You can place blue and orange portals
- Portals must be placed on wall tiles
- Portals must be within your line of sight
- Only one portal of each color can exist at a time
- Walking into one portal teleports you to the other portal
- Placing a new portal removes the old portal of the same color

Available tools:
- SpawnPlayer: Creates a player and returns a ref you'll use for all actions
- ObserveWorld: Shows the current state of the game world around you
- MovePlayer: Moves the player one tile at a time (+1, 0, or -1 for x and y)
- PlacePortal: Places a blue or orange portal at specific tile coordinates
- GetPortals: Shows where portals are currently placed

Your goal: Explore the world, place portals strategically to solve puzzles, and reach the win tile!

Start by spawning a player, observing the world, and then experiment with portal placement.
